01

Research

I am interested in AI. In particular, I want to enable AI to make creative contributions to science—for instance, discovering new statistical algorithms for complex experimental data, and developing a scientific understanding of AI systems themselves.

I also want to understand the societal and economic impact of AI and what it means for humanity—where the unique value of human intelligence lies, and how we should interact and co-evolve with this new form of intelligence.
